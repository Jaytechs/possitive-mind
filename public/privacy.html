Privacy

Positive Mind stores your journal entries and preferences locally in your browser on this device. The app does not send them to a server. There are no accounts, analytics, advertising trackers, or third-party requests.

Your data is not encrypted by this app. Anyone with access to your unlocked device and browser may be able to read it. You can export your entries from Settings or delete them from this device there.

Your browser or device may apply its own storage, backup, or deletion policies. Export a copy if you need to keep your entries.
